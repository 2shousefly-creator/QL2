# Visual Quality 2 prompt set

Tool: built-in imagegen (not the API CLI). Final assets are under
`public/assets/ngu-dai-de-vuong/{H001,H017,B001}/spine-v2` and
`public/assets/ngu-dai-de-vuong/vfx/quality-v2`.

All character prompts: premium high-detail oriental fantasy 2.5D RPG, full body,
three-quarter battle stance facing right, truly transparent background, readable
face and natural proportions, distinct material textures, volumetric gradient
shading and subtle rim lighting, complete silhouette, no text/scenery/clip art.

- H001 Kình Sơn: imposing stone guardian, carved mature face, weathered stone,
  bronze armor integrated with rock, moss/jade runes, detailed forward shield.
- H017 Minh Châu: elegant adult phoenix mage, layered vermilion silk robes,
  soft auburn/black hair with flame tips, gold filigree, jade, ornate phoenix staff.
- B001 Thạch Giáp Cự Tướng: dark basalt giant general, oxidized bronze and iron
  lamellar armor, amber fissures, horned crown, heavy stone mace, worn red cloth.

All final animated sheet prompts: SQUARE canvas, actual alpha transparency,
exactly 6 columns x 4 rows, 24 sequential row-major frames, isolated centered
sprites in equal rectangular cells with transparent margins, no matte/scenery,
no grid or labels. Detailed volumetric fantasy VFX.

- H001-1: spark, erupting stone and jade energy, falling debris, fading dust.
- H001-2: mountain rune, assembling gold edge, jade/gold barrier dome rising,
  circulating rune highlights, dissolving light shards.
- H017-1: tiny contact spark, curling orange ignition, white-hot spherical peak,
  dark smoke, dim embers and faint residual wisps.
- H017-2: descending meteor contact, impact spike opens into phoenix wings,
  phoenix silhouette and shockwave, dispersing flame, fading ground fire.
